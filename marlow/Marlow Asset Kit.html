Using Marlow

Do

  • Use SVG wherever it scales — web, FAB, avatars. Reach for PNG only where raster is required (favicons, app stores, email).
  • Keep clear space around him equal to the olive — don't crowd the rim.
  • Place the mascot on dark or photographic grounds; use the green badge when you need a contained shape.
  • Minimum size: 24px for the badge, 40px for the bare mascot (the stem thins out below that).

Don't

  • Don't recolor the glass or swap the amber pour — the green/cream/amber trio is the recognition.
  • Don't stretch or rotate (except the built-in “cheers” tilt) or add outer drop-shadows on top of the baked-in ones.
  • Don't place the bare mascot on a busy light background where the cream face disappears — use a badge or sticker instead.
  • Don't redraw the face by hand — use the expression set so every mood stays on-model.
